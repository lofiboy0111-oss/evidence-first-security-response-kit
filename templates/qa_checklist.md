# Customer Response QA Checklist

## Completeness

- [ ] Every question, sub-question, attachment, and required field is addressed.
- [ ] Scope is consistent across product, environment, region, and date.
- [ ] Blank, pending, and not-applicable answers are explained.

## Evidence

- [ ] Every substantive answer has a citation or explicit reason it cannot be cited.
- [ ] Sources are current enough for the question.
- [ ] Prior answers were not used as the sole evidence.
- [ ] Contradictions are resolved or escalated.

## Claims and risk

- [ ] No unsupported absolute claims such as “always,” “never,” or “100%.”
- [ ] No new contractual, legal, security, privacy, uptime, or remediation promise was introduced.
- [ ] High-risk questions have subject-matter-owner approval.
- [ ] Restricted evidence is not disclosed without authorization.

## Handling and release

- [ ] Customer data stayed in approved storage and tools.
- [ ] No credentials, secrets, unnecessary personal data, or hidden drafts are included.
- [ ] Final recipient, sender, attachments, format, deadline, and approval are confirmed.
- [ ] Final version and approval are recorded in the audit receipt.
- [ ] Temporary files and access are deleted or retained under the documented policy.

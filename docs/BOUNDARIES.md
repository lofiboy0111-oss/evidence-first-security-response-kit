# Safety and Usage Boundaries

This kit is intentionally local and deterministic. It does not upload files, call a model provider, submit answers, or contact customers.

It is not:

- legal advice;
- an audit opinion;
- a security certification;
- a compliance determination;
- a substitute for a qualified security, privacy, legal, or compliance professional; or
- a guarantee that any questionnaire answer is accurate or accepted.

Users must review every output, verify every evidence citation, and follow their own contractual, privacy, security, and retention requirements. Do not process secrets, regulated data, or restricted customer materials unless the environment and policies explicitly permit it.

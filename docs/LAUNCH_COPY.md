Security questionnaires keep turning into the same operational problem: hundreds of repetitive questions, evidence scattered across folders, and unclear approval trails.

I built a small open workflow to help with the mechanical part:

- import a questionnaire CSV;
- classify likely categories;
- flag priority and evidence needs;
- maintain an evidence register; and
- run a human QA/release checklist.
